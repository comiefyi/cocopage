About Me

Where data meets purpose.

Zijing (Coco) Guo is an M.P.A. candidate at Columbia University's School of International and Public Affairs, concentrating in Climate, Energy & Environment with a specialization in Data Analytics & Quantitative Analysis. Her work sits at the intersection of ESG reporting, energy systems, and corporate sustainability strategy.

She currently works as an Analyst at Wood Mackenzie and as a Product Manager at an AI Tennis Matching Platform startup, while concurrently serving as a Fellow at Advanced Energy Group, supporting ESG data reporting, KPI development, and decarbonization strategies. Previously, she interned with Deloitte in Shanghai on SAP digital transformation projects, and conducted research at UC Davis on rural connectivity and sustainability policy using R and Stata.

Coco is passionate about using data-driven insights to help organizations achieve measurable progress toward their environmental and social goals.
